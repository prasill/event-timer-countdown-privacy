Privacy Policy — Event Timer Countdown

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Event Timer Countdown is a Windows event timer application.

Information collected

Event Timer Countdown does not collect, sell, or send personal information to the developer. The application does not use advertising, analytics, tracking cookies, or online user accounts.

Local settings

Timer settings, presets, display preferences, and Companion settings may be saved locally on your Windows computer. These settings remain on your computer unless you delete them.

Network features

If you enable Companion control or NDI output, the application communicates over your local network using the settings you provide. These features are optional and are disabled unless you enable them.

The application does not transmit this information to the developer.

Third-party services

The application may include links to third-party websites, such as NDI documentation. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
